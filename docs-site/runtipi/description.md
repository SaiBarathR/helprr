# Helprr

Sonarr, Radarr, qBittorrent, Jellyfin and more in one app you install on your phone,
with push notifications.

Helprr makes its own passwords and keys the first time it starts. Open it, create your
admin, then connect your apps in **Settings → Instances**.

Guide: https://saibarathr.github.io/helprr/install/runtipi/
